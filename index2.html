Workout Information

Workout Journal
Each exercise logs up to 4 sets per session. Weighted exercises use one fixed weight per exercise — edit it inline in the Open Table — so you just log reps against it; the trend chart marks the point where that weight changed. Bodyweight and timed exercises log reps or seconds directly. Sets count toward a 24-sets/month goal per exercise, shown live while you fill out the table.
Strength Rating
Each workout's rating compares your recent performance to your own personal best, per exercise — not to anyone else's numbers. For each exercise: average your last 3 logged sessions, divide by your all-time best session — that's the exercise's ratio (100% = right at your peak). Those ratios combine using the weight % shown next to each exercise below. An exercise needs at least 2 logged sessions to count; until then it's excluded and its weight is redistributed across whichever exercises do have data.
Add Additional Workout Today
You can log one extra workout session on the same day — e.g. Upper A in the morning, Lower B at night — from the Exercise page. It gets its own workout type, rounds, and Open Table, and its rounds/sets count toward that split's monthly totals and Strength Rating. It never affects that day's Exercise Score, cardio selection, or rest-day limits. If the same exercise shows up in both sessions on one day, their logged sets are simply added together.
